NFL Health System Sponsorship Map

27 Teams. $176 Million. Zero Independent Physicians.

Nonprofit health systems are spending nine figures on NFL branding deals while claiming community benefit on their 990s. Every dollar is a marketing line item. Not one of these partnerships funds independent physician practice. Here's every deal, every system, every team.

03 The Pattern

The Same Systems. The Same Playbook.

UPMC owns the Steelers' training facility. Jefferson Health's logo is on Eagles practice jerseys. CommonSpirit has deals with both the 49ers and the Broncos. Ascension sponsors the Colts and the Titans. These are not local partnerships. They are national brand consolidation strategies executed by nonprofit systems operating under tax-exempt status.

Sponsorship contracts are separate from medical care provision. A team can have one health system providing medical care while accepting corporate sponsorships from a completely different hospital. The "official healthcare partner" designation is marketing. The physicians treating the players often come from different systems entirely.

Every one of these systems files a Form 990. Every one claims community benefit. The question is whether a multi-million-dollar NFL branding deal qualifies.
